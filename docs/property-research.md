## 5. Nearby Attractions & Points of Interest

1. **Agonda Beach (0 km / Immediate Access):** 3-km stretch of golden sand, gentle waves, Olive Ridley sea turtle protected nesting site, serene beach shacks.
2. **Cola Beach & Freshwater Lagoon (5.5 km / ~15 mins):** Famous secluded beach featuring a hidden emerald freshwater stream meeting the ocean.
3. **Butterfly Beach (9.5 km / ~25 mins drive or boat):** High-biodiversity cove surrounded by steep cliffs, renowned for dolphin sightings and privacy.
4. **Cabo de Rama Fort (14.5 km / ~30 mins):** Historic coastal fortress with panoramic ocean ramparts dating back to Soonda kings and Portuguese maritime history.
5. **Palolem Beach & Green Island (9 km / ~20 mins):** Famous crescent-shaped bay with calm swimming waters, kayak tours, yoga studios, and beachside dining.
6. **Canacona / Chaudi Town & Fish Market (10 km / ~18 mins):** Local cultural hub, spice markets, ATM hubs, and traditional Goan bakers (Poder).
